# Influencer-kit: Laid Off to Interview-Ready in 30 Days

## Wie zoek je?
Accounts met **10.000 tot 50.000 volgers** die werkzoekenden helpen: carrièrecoaches, recruiters, cv-schrijvers, accounts met "job search tips" en LinkedIn-coaches.
Engelstalig publiek (VS, VK, Canada, Australië). Minstens 3% engagement, controleren op **socialblade.com**.

## Bericht 1: eerste contact (DM of reactie op een story)
> Hi [name]! Love your interview tips, especially the one about [specific post]. I have a commission-based partnership idea that fits your audience really well. Open to hearing about it?

## Bericht 2: als ze ja zeggen
> Thanks! I created a guide for people who were laid off and dread the "Why did you leave?" question: *Laid Off to Interview-Ready in 30 Days* (30-second answer scripts, résumé-gap answers, a 30-day plan and worksheets).
>
> The offer: you get **40% of every sale** through your own affiliate link. No upfront cost, no minimums. I'll send you a free copy so you can check it first, plus ready-made story ideas.
>
> Could you share roughly how many story views you get, and where most of your audience is based?

## Bericht 3: opvolgen (na 1 dag, maximaal 2 keer)
> Hi [name], just bumping this in case it got buried. Happy to send the free copy so you can take a look, no strings attached.

## Na een deal
1. Maak in Payhip een affiliate-link voor ze aan (**Settings → Affiliates**).
2. Stuur ze **een gratis exemplaar** en de story-ideeën hieronder.
3. Vraag ze om **#ad** of "paid partnership" te gebruiken. Dat is wettelijk verplicht.

## 3 story-ideeën voor de influencer
**Story 1: poll**
> "Has 'Why did you leave your last job?' ever made you freeze in an interview?" [Poll: Yes 😬 / No]

**Story 2: de tip**
> "Here's a 30-second answer that works: 1) what happened, 2) why it wasn't about you, 3) where you're going. Then STOP talking."

**Story 3: de link**
> "If you were laid off, this guide walks you through it in 30 days, with scripts, résumé-gap answers and worksheets. Link here 👇 #ad"

**Niet doen:** nep-schaarste ("only 10 spots left!") of beloftes zoals "guaranteed job".
