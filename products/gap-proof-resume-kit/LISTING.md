## Productbeschrijving (Engels)

**Worried the gap on your résumé is costing you interviews?**

After a layoff, every month without a job feels like another mark against you. You look at those dates and wonder what the recruiter will think, and how on earth to explain it.

This kit helps you explain your gap in **one calm, honest line**, fill it with what you actually did, and make the rest of your résumé strong enough that the gap becomes a footnote.

**What's inside:**
- **3 ATS-friendly résumé templates** (Classic 1-page, Experienced 2-page, Career Change). Single column, no tables or graphics, easy for applicant tracking systems to read
- **3 cover letter templates**: recent layoff, longer gap (6+ months), and career pivot
- **The Gap Explanation Bank**: 40 fill-in lines for 10 common reasons (layoff, long search, caregiving, health, studying, freelance, relocation, career change, parental leave, planned break). Each comes as a résumé line, a cover letter sentence, LinkedIn wording and a 20-second interview answer, so your story is consistent everywhere
- **Gap-time worksheet**: turn courses, freelance work and volunteering into real résumé experience
- **Bullet-rewrite formula** with before-and-after examples
- **ATS checklist and keyword-matching worksheet**
- **LinkedIn "Open to Work"** headline and About fill-ins
- **A full before-and-after example résumé**
- **28-page guide** with a "résumé in one evening" plan

**Format:** instant download (ZIP). Templates are .docx files that open in **Microsoft Word, Google Docs and Pages**. Guide is a PDF.

**Who it's for:** anyone with a gap after a layoff, a career break or a long job search.

*This is a digital product. Nothing will be shipped. For personal use only. Results depend on your experience and the jobs you apply for.*
